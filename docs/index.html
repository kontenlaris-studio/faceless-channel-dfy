FAQ

Do you guarantee views, subscribers, or revenue?
No — and be wary of anyone who does. Views depend on the platform and the audience. What we guarantee is consistent, on-schedule production in an approved format: the part that is actually in anyone's control. We also make no promise about monetization eligibility — that is each platform's decision, not ours.

Do I need to appear on camera or record my voice?
No. The format is fully faceless: animated frames, burned-in captions, and TTS narration or captioned-silent delivery. There is no lip-sync — if you need a talking avatar with lip-sync, this is not that service.

Who owns the channel and the videos?
You do. The channel is created and owned by you, and every video we deliver is yours — including after you cancel.

How do videos get uploaded?
Two ways: we deliver the finished files and you upload them, or you grant us upload access and we post to the agreed schedule. We never need your password — access is granted through the platform's own permission features, or you upload yourself.

Is the content original?
Yes — scripts and visuals are generated by our pipeline for your channel. We do not re-upload other creators' footage or copyrighted material.

What languages?
Indonesian or English — scripts, captions and titles in your channel's language, chosen at intake.

Can I cancel?
Anytime. The retainer is month-to-month; videos already delivered stay yours, and the monthly report covers everything published up to cancellation.
